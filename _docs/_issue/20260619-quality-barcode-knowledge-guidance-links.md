# 2026-06-19 Issue: Quality Barcode Knowledge Guidance Links

## Problem
The barcode quality knowledge reuse panel allowed publishing a CAPA knowledge sample without local guidance or evidence navigation at the point of action.

## Impact
- Operators had to infer the required checks before turning rework evidence into reusable quality knowledge.
- The UI did not directly connect the action to audit replay, report evidence, or help.
- The flow did not fully satisfy the core product principles for multilingual support, contextual links, and complex-operation guidance.

## Resolution
- Added bilingual guidance explaining the checks before publishing quality knowledge.
- Added links to audit replay, report evidence, and the panel help anchor.
- Added unit coverage and real browser evidence for the rendered guidance.

## Status
Closed for the barcode quality knowledge reuse panel with unit, coverage, lint, build, and browser evidence.
